# Cosmotune 1.0.28

This update keeps you signed in to YouTube on the desktop when your PC will not let Cosmotune save the sign-in, shows the lists of
Watch add-ons that name their own kind (such as CNCVerse's films, series and anime), and keeps your place in Watch search.

## Highlights

- <!-- icon: check --> **Stays signed in** — A blocked save no longer signs you out.
- <!-- icon: look --> **More add-on rows** — Anime and other add-on lists show on Home.
- <!-- icon: settings --> **Search keeps its place** — Coming back finds your search.

## Desktop

- Signing in to YouTube no longer ends a few seconds later on a PC that refuses to save the sign-in. Some security software
  guards saved browser sign-ins and refuses the write without saying so; Cosmotune then read back an older, expired sign-in and
  put it in force, and YouTube rejected it at once. Cosmotune now checks that the sign-in was saved: when it was not, it keeps
  the one you just made for as long as the app runs and says "This PC blocked saving it", so you sign in again after a restart.
  The log says when a save was refused.
- An update whose install script could not be written ("Access is denied") now writes it under a name of its own, or in the
  temporary folder, and the log says why the first try was refused.

## Watch (phone and desktop)

- Add-ons that call their lists "other" or "anime" rather than films or series (CNCVerse's HDHub4U, Netflix, Re:ANIME, GoTaku
  and others) now show as rows on Home. A title from such a list opens as a film, or as a series with its seasons and episodes
  when it has them, and plays from the add-on. Before, those lists were left out.
- A series opened from such a list is called a series at the top of its page and lights Series in the sidebar.
- A channel that several sources list (Sun TV from the free channel list and from an add-on) is one channel in Live TV and
  search, and its copies from each source are under Sources when it plays. Before, each source's copy was a channel of its own.
  A channel with HD in its name, or of another country, stays its own.
- Coming back from a title or the player to Search finds the same search and results, not an empty page.
- Copies an add-on sends encrypted (some live TV channels) are not played: Cosmotune does not unlock a broadcaster's protection.
