# Cosmotune 1.0.22

## Highlights

- <!-- icon: lyrics --> **Lyrics, timed and styled** — Six styles, and timing for many more songs.
- <!-- icon: watch --> **Picture in picture** — Keep films and videos playing as you browse.
- <!-- icon: look --> **Light or dark** — Switch the theme from your profile menu.
- <!-- icon: playback --> **Music videos that fit** — The right video for more of your songs.
- <!-- icon: new --> **Watch, grown up** — Profiles, playlists, trailers and more.

## Lyrics and Now Playing

- **Six lyric styles**, chosen with the Style chip in Lyrics, for every song or just one:
  - **Orbit Read**: the line being sung in bold, with a moon tracing the singer's place.
  - **Orbital**: the line on the ring around the cover.
  - **Stretch**: a held note widens the word.
  - **Call & Answer**: each singer on their own side. It turns on by itself for duets.
  - **Pulse**: the words breathe with the music.
  - **Poster**: each line as a poster in the cover's colour.
- **TV:** has the Style option too, with Poster as an ambient screen after two minutes without the remote.
- **Share video:** the lyric share video can be saved as Poster, Stretch or Orbital.
- **Album titles:** set in a style that suits the album on the album page, the song card, the mini player, TV and the watch.
- **Smoother playback visuals:**
  - The ring and moon around the cover glide instead of stepping, in every place they appear.
  - The volume never disappears from the desktop song card. At narrow widths, shuffle and repeat move into a menu instead of hiding under Play.
- **Background colour:** the background takes the same colour as the song card, so a red cover gives a red page rather than brown.
- **More timed lyrics:** YouTube Music lyrics now come with timing, so many more songs, Tamil and other Indian songs among them, follow the music line by line. A timed answer always wins over plain text.
- **Fewer, working lyric sources:** sources that no longer answer, or only repeat another, are gone. Five remain. Saved source choices carry over.
- **Poster style:** Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Hindi and Tamil lines no longer overlap, and long lines without spaces break into even rows.
- **No titles as lyrics:** a song or video title sent as the first lyric line is dropped.

## Picture in picture

- **Phone:** films, series, live TV and music videos keep playing in a small window when you leave the app or press the picture-in-picture button. The window has play, pause, next and previous.
- **Desktop:** Watch and music videos get the same small always-on-top window as Video, and it stays open while you browse.
- **Android TV:** can use picture in picture on TVs that support it.
- **Stays playing:** films and music videos keep playing in the small window after you leave their page.
- **Live channels:** the picture stays centred when a channel changes its picture shape.

## Video

- **Back goes back:** tapping a related or Up next video replaces the one playing. Back, the close arrow or Esc returns you to where you started, and the video carries on in the mini player.
- **Videos start faster:** signed in, a video now starts in about a second instead of up to half a minute. Resting the pointer on a video gets it ready before you click, and the next video is prepared before the current one ends.
- **No ads in lists:** ads, sponsored cards and YouTube Premium promos are kept out of Home, Search, Up next, channels and playlists.

## Theme

- **Desktop:** has a Theme setting (System, Light or Dark) and Pure black, and follows Windows when set to System.
- **Quick switch:** a sun, moon and auto switch in the profile menu on phone and desktop.

## Music videos

- **Official uploads:** videos from labels and TV channels (such as Sun TV) are now found, even for short film names.
- **Wrong videos refused:** reaction, review, cover and podcast videos are never chosen.
- **Faster searches:** the playing song's search goes first and waits longer, so a slow answer is no longer taken as "no video".
- **Matches kept:** a video found for a song is remembered, so it stays when Cosmotune switches to a better copy of the song.

## Watch

- **Profiles and lists:** viewer profiles with an optional PIN, each with its own lists, playlists and preferences.
- **Sync:** opt-in sync of progress, My list, playlists and preferences.
- **Home:** Top 10 and more rows, filters by type, genre and year, and a Get started card.
- **Title pages and playback:**
  - Trailers, and optional IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes and Metacritic ratings.
  - Single subtitle files from SubDL, and skip intro and recap for anime.
  - Casting films to TVs and speakers on your network.
- **Downloads:** check free space first and let you pick where they go.
- **Plugins:** add-on scripts can be installed, pinned and checked for updates.
- **Optional services:** sign in to Trakt or Simkl to sync your watchlist and history, or add a Real-Debrid, AllDebrid or TorBox account.

## Music

- **Lyrics romanisation:** offline romanisation for Korean, Japanese kana, Chinese and other scripts.
- **Headphones:** EQ profiles for 39 headphone models, and import of GraphicEQ and APO files.
- **Gapless albums:** albums play gapless when you play them in order.
- **Crash notes:** short crash notes are kept on the device (never uploaded) for you to read or share.

## Also

- **What's new:** shows a few highlights in plain words, with a link to these full notes.
- **Spacing:** fixed across the app, wherever text and buttons sat too close.

## Release limits

- **Sound alignment:** automatic sound alignment when Cosmotune upgrades a song's quality is built but switched off until it has been tried on real music.
- **Accounts:** Trakt, Simkl, AllDebrid and TorBox have not been tried with real accounts. Trakt and Simkl need their public app IDs registered before they work.
- **Casting:** DLNA casting of films has not been tried on a real TV. Google Cast for Watch is not built.
- **TV picture in picture:** not tried on a real TV.
- **Windows window effect:** stays slightly dark in the light theme.
- **Lyrics for your own files:** Genius is no longer a source, so a local file without lyrics inside it gets plain lyrics only from LRCLIB.
