# Cosmotune 1.0.17

A new look across the whole app, called Orbit, a new Watch mode for films, series and live TV, and a set of new music features.

## Highlights

- <!-- icon: look --> **A whole new look** — Round covers, and colours from your music.
- <!-- icon: watch --> **Films, series and TV** — Free films, series and live channels.
- <!-- icon: live --> **Live now** — Follow your team's score from a small pill.
- <!-- icon: channels --> **Indian TV channels** — DD channels and live news in six languages.
- <!-- icon: timer --> **Reliable sleep timer** — Stops on time, even with the screen off.

## Orbit, the new look

- One design everywhere: Music, Watch, Video, Podio, Manga, Read, Settings, rooms, the phone dock, the desktop bar and navigation, TV and Wear.
- Covers are round discs. Where a song is up to is the orbit drawn round its disc, with a small moon at the current second.
- Each page leads with one colour card in its own cover's colour, with the play button standing in a round bite cut from its corner.
- Nothing is drawn on top of a cover or a video: play buttons sit on the edge, and lengths, LIVE and download marks sit under the picture.
- The whole app takes the playing song's colours again while "Colours follow the cover" is on, in light and dark.
- Home has a layout for each kind of shelf (song rows, records, a featured card, colour cards, ranked charts, artist portraits).
- Artist pages play Apple's moving artist portrait when one exists, and add music videos, live performances and the artist's signature. Album pages, Home's top card, the search top result and Now Playing's record play animated covers where they exist. All of them follow the animated artwork, reduced motion and mobile-data settings.

## Now Playing and videos

- Music videos are shown whole by default, never cropped to a tall frame. The space round a video gets a soft glow from its own colours.
- A screen button in the song card offers Whole, Fill, Cinema and Off. Cinema gives the window to the video, with the card floating at the foot and hiding after three seconds. Press F or double-click to enter it and Esc to leave. On a phone, turn it sideways. The choice is remembered and shared with Video mode.
- Up next uses the same rows as Home, with drag to reorder and remove.

## Watch: films, series and live TV

- A new mode (Ctrl+3 on desktop) with Home, Films, Series, Live TV with a programme guide, Search, My list, Downloads and Plugins.
- It plays out of the box from built-in plugins, each with an on/off switch: free live channels (iptv-org, Pluto TV, Samsung TV Plus, Plex), free films and series (Plex, the Internet Archive, official free YouTube channels), and Stremio's Public Domain Movies, OpenSubtitles and WatchHub add-ons.
- Add more sources yourself: Stremio and Nuvio add-on links, your own Jellyfin, Emby or Plex server, M3U and Xtream playlists with guides, and local folders. Install links from add-on websites open in Cosmotune. Plugin packs can be shared by link or QR code and offered in a room, and your plugins sync across devices with cloud sync.
- Films, episodes and channels can be watched together in a room. Each person's app finds the same title in its own sources; it says so if their copy is a different cut.
- Film and series details come from TMDB. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
- No torrent, debrid or pirate-site plugins are built in.

## Live now

- A Live pill on every mode shows the score of the match or show you follow and how many more are live. It opens Live now: live matches and shows, what's coming up with a reminder, and what just finished.
- Watch is offered only where a free stream carries the event: DD Sports, DD National or a broadcaster's own YouTube live stream.
- Cricket scores come from CricketData.org and football scores from football-data.org, each with a free key; without one, TheSportsDB gives the next and last match only.
- Settings → Live now turns the pill on or off, picks your sports and follows teams.

## Indian channels

- Prasar Bharati (DD): DD News, DD India, DD National, DD Sports, DD Kisan, DD Urdu, DD Bharati, DD Tamil and the regional DD channels, played from Prasar Bharati's own YouTube channels.
- Live on YouTube: the broadcasters' own live news streams in Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Kannada, Hindi and English, and Sansad TV.
- Pay channels such as Sun TV, Vijay TV and Zee Tamil are not built in. Find plugins in Watch links to plugin directories so you can add what you have access to.

## New music features

- Automatic lyrics timing: Cosmotune listens for the vocals and lines the synced lyrics up, on top of the manual offset.
- Spatial audio with crossfeed, virtual speakers, binaural 5.1/7.1 and mono, replacing the old stereo widener.
- Song recognition (off until you turn it on; it uses Shazam's unofficial service and sends only a fingerprint).
- YouTube captions as the last lyrics source; "About this album" from Wikipedia.
- A maximum volume, pitch separate from speed, a proxy setting and an offline mode.
- Import playlists from Apple Music and Deezer links as well as Spotify.
- Cast to TVs and speakers on your network (DLNA).
- Save synced lyrics as a short video for stories.
- Several of these are ported from Mochy Music (GPL-3.0); see [NOTICE.md](../../NOTICE.md).

## Fixes

- The sleep timer now pauses on time with the screen off; before, it could run on for hours. It also pauses casting, rooms, Video and Watch.
- Now Playing shows a pill with the sleep timer's time left, and pills for the features in use.
- Space plays and pauses on the desktop.
- The desktop no longer crashes when a song's stream and its moving artwork open together.
- Watch and Read follow the light theme.
- Queue edits are kept during a crossfade or a version swap.
- TV: Watch uses less memory, and several focus problems are fixed.

## Release limits

- Rooms, voice and Watch together have still not been tried by several people on real devices.
- Watch: Plex's free catalogue and some live services were checked only against test servers. Pluto TV has no on-demand films in India. The iptv-org channels have no programme guide unless you add a guide address.
- Casting doesn't yet send songs stored only on the phone, and the desktop doesn't move on to the next song while casting.
- Desktop install links are registered with the "Set as handler" button on Windows and Linux; the installers do not register them yet.
- The TV app cannot join rooms yet.
- Live now reminders only fire while the app is running. On the phone, the Live pill shows on the Music and Video home pages, not on every page.
- DD channels other than DD News, DD India, DD Kisan and DD Urdu are live only during programmes.
- The new design was checked in the running desktop app and on phone, TV and Wear emulators, not on real phones, TVs or watches.
