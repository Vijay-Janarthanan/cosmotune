# Cosmotune 1.0.30

This update fixes song video matching: Cosmotune now plays a song's real video more often, and less often a still picture or
someone's shortened copy. On Windows, your sign-in now stays after a restart or an update.

## Highlights

- <!-- icon: look --> **Better song videos** — The real video is found more often.
- <!-- icon: check --> **No more still pictures** — A still poster is not taken for a video.
- <!-- icon: settings --> **Stays signed in** — Your sign-in survives restarts and updates.

## Song videos (phone and desktop)

- Fixed song video matching. Uploads from a film's own channel are tried first, even when the channel has no verified
  badge.
- An upload that matches only part of the song (one that starts late or skips a verse) is shown only when no other upload
  matches more of it. A song that had such a video before is looked up again.
- A still picture uploaded with the song as its sound is told apart from a video by YouTube's preview frames, so it is no
  longer shown as the song's video, and a real video is no longer mistaken for one.
- A video search that YouTube answers with an empty page is asked once more, so fewer songs end up without a video.

## Desktop

- Windows: sign-ins are now kept, encrypted for your Windows account, in Cosmotune's own data folder. Some PCs refused to save
  them where Cosmotune kept them before, so the app asked you to sign in again after every restart or update. Sign in once
  more after this update and it stays.
