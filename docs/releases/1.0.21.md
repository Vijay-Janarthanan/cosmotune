# Cosmotune 1.0.21

- Movie and series pages now show an About section with the full plot and available ratings, credits, cast and related titles before playback.
- Watch keeps the plot and rating from the catalogue card when the detail response omits them. For films, the source picker remains available if the full details fail to load.
- Series pages retain season and episode navigation, and the play shortcut appears once a next episode is available.
