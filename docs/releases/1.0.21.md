# Cosmotune 1.0.21

## Highlights

- <!-- icon: playlist --> **Bring your playlists** — Import playlists from Spotify, Apple Music and more.
- <!-- icon: check --> **Your order, kept** — Same name and order, and a note of any missing songs.
- <!-- icon: sync --> **On all your devices** — Imported playlists sync with your Cosmotune account.

## Details

- On Android, choose Cosmotune when sharing a public playlist from Spotify, Amazon Music, Apple Music, Deezer or YouTube Music. On Windows, macOS and Linux, paste the link in **Import a playlist** or open a Cosmotune import link.
- The imported playlist keeps its name and the order of the tracks Cosmotune can verify. Cosmotune reports unmatched tracks and tracks the source indicates it withheld.
- The playlist is saved to the Cosmotune library. Sign in to the same Cosmotune account with playlist sync enabled to have it appear across devices.
