# Cosmotune 1.0.31

A new look across every mode, with the same header, rows, navigation and full-screen player in Music, Video, Watch, Podio,
Read and Manga. Song videos now sit in the middle of Now Playing with a soft glow round them, the screen stays on while a
video plays, and playback picks itself up again when the network drops.

## Highlights

- <!-- icon: look --> **A cleaner look everywhere** — One design for every mode and page.
- <!-- icon: playback --> **Videos fit better** — Song videos sit centred in a soft glow.
- <!-- icon: lyrics --> **Lyric line** — The line being sung shows above the seek bar.
- <!-- icon: check --> **Screen stays on** — It no longer turns off while a video plays.
- <!-- icon: download --> **Smaller downloads** — The Windows installer is a quarter smaller.

## Now Playing

- A video sits in the middle of the page at its own shape, with a soft glow of its own picture above and below it instead of
  empty space. Pinch to switch between the whole video and the video filling the space; double-tap for Cinema.
- The line of the lyrics being sung shows just above the seek bar; tap it to open the full lyrics.
- The sound quality is back, between the times on the seek bar.
- Play sits in the middle of the controls, and the screen choice is in the top row.
- Choose a song's video yourself when the one found is not the one you want.
- Sideways, a tap shows only the title, the seek bar and the playback buttons, not the song card.
- Full screen keeps hold for 2x speed, brightness and volume drags, and picture-in-picture.

## Every mode

- One page header, flat rows and one bottom bar in every mode; on a tablet or a wide window the bar becomes a side rail.
- Removing something from a list can be undone.
- In Music, Search is a round button next to the bottom bar, one tap away from every page.
- Playlists can be kept downloaded: new songs are saved on Wi-Fi.
- Video, Watch, Podio, Read and Manga pages follow the new design, and Read and Manga show the music player when music is
  playing.
- Podcasts get a queue, chapters and transcripts in the player, and the player opens fully.

## Playback

- The phone's screen stays on while a video plays or loads, as in any video player. Paused or audio only, it turns off as
  usual.
- When the network drops, playback says it is reconnecting and carries on by itself once the network is back. A song stuck
  loading is retried.
- Lyrics are kept on your device for a month, so a song you played before shows its lyrics straight away.

## Desktop

- Videos on desktop use about half the processor they did.
- The Windows installer is about a quarter smaller, and the phone app is a little smaller too.
- The built-in Tor connection is removed. Your own proxy setting still works.
