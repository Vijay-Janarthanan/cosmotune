# Cosmotune 1.0.34

A big release for every mode: write and time your own lyrics, sound effects for music, a much fuller book reader with
audiobooks, subtitles and torrents in Watch, live chat and transcripts in Video, and a new Spotlight with live scores and the
world's news.

## Highlights

- <!-- icon: lyrics --> **Your own lyrics** — Search, write and time lyrics for any song.
- <!-- icon: audio --> **Sound effects** — 8D, 3D room, reverb and a vocal remover.
- <!-- icon: read --> **A fuller book reader** — Highlight, take notes, look up and read aloud.
- <!-- icon: watch --> **Subtitles in Watch** — Shown on every device, in the look you pick.
- <!-- icon: live --> **A new Spotlight** — Live scores with scorers and the world's news.

## Every mode

- Search is the round button beside the tabs in every mode, on one search page with your recent searches.
- Spotlight, the line at the top of every mode, shows a live match you follow or the world's biggest story. Open it for Top,
  World, your country, Football, Cricket and Coming up; a match opens its timeline, stats and line-ups or the scorecard.
- Every icon button names itself: on hover or keyboard focus on the desktop, with its shortcut, and on a long press on
  the phone.
- After skipping releases, What's new also tells you what each skipped one brought.
- Look and colours has Interface size and Text font, and Home sections, to take any shelf off Home.

## Music

- Lyrics: search for the right lyrics by hand, or type or paste your own and tap along to time them, line by line or word by
  word, with duet lines. Lyrics can also come from a music video's own captions.
- Romanised lyrics can show on their own, without the original script above them.
- Settings, Sound, Sound effects: 8D, 3D room, echo, room and hall reverb, chorus, vocal remover and Night mode. Volume boost
  plays quiet songs louder, and a song can keep its own sound effect beside its own speed and key.
- The equaliser saves your own curves by name, takes an exact value per band, and reads and writes AutoEQ presets.
- The seek line becomes the song's waveform once you have heard it, with dots where listeners commented on a moment.
- Repeat a song two, three, five or ten times from the player's More menu, then the queue carries on.
- Bookmarks keep moments of a song, repeat a part from A to B, and jump to a local file's own chapters.
- Mix view shows a song's tempo and key and where Automix blends it in and out.
- Fresh shuffle plays the songs you heard lately further back; Local Music sorts by tempo and has Smooth shuffle.
- Best part skips to the part people replay most, or to the first chorus from the lyrics.
- Rate songs with stars, give them moods and tags, and play them from Library's Moods and tags.
- Playlists go into folders in Library, and each album and playlist page keeps the order you chose.
- Block an artist from a song's menu and hide explicit songs; AutoPlay, radio and Home leave them out.
- Read and write a song's YouTube comments from the player's More menu.
- Paste a YouTube or YouTube Music link into Search and it plays or opens.
- Finding a song in your library forgives a typo or a missing accent.
- Radio sits at the top of Explore with your mixes, in every YouTube language.
- Song info shows the album, length, credits, first release, ISRC and genres from MusicBrainz.
- Replay has Memories, a day-of-the-week chart, time per source, and a story video to share.
- History groups songs by day, week or month and by where they came from.
- Bring your Spotify or ListenBrainz listening history into Replay, and likes and playlists from InnerTune, OuterTune,
  Metrolist, ViMusic, RiMusic, Kreate or SimpMusic backups.
- Restore lost likes finds songs YouTube's Liked music dropped and likes them again.
- Save the queue, your liked songs or a playlist as a CSV file.
- Smart downloads keep your 25, 50 or 100 most played songs downloaded. Liked songs download within a minute when Liked
  Music is kept downloaded, and downloads keep a history.
- Get a notification when an artist you follow puts out something new.
- Local Music has a Folders view, hides short songs or whole folders, and sorts numbers the way people count.
- Play from your own Jellyfin server, and share songs from your Subsonic server as its public link.
- Back up automatically every day or week, to a folder or your own WebDAV server.
- YouTube Premium listeners can stream the clearer 256 kbps Opus.
- Skip the talking in music videos, and move a music video earlier or later against the sound.
- Settings can tell other apps what is playing (your webhook, music broadcasts, a text file for OBS) and use private DNS.
- Keep the screen on while the player is open, or whenever music plays.
- Rooms: with Only the host controls on, the host can make someone a co-host who can also control the music.
- Up next marks liked and downloaded songs, and has plainer rows with room between them.
- Imported playlists can be renamed, deleted and edited, and long playlists load to the end.

## Video

- Live and finished streams have a Chat tab, and videos with captions a Transcript tab.
- Channel pages have Search, Posts, Podcasts, Courses and Releases tabs, Hide watched, and a bell for new videos.
- Subscriptions has channel groups and can follow PeerTube channels in the same feed.
- Filters hide videos, channels and comments by words; Family-friendly only and Fewer distractions hide more.
- A Next chapter button, a touch lock in full screen, Volume and Voice boost, and Skip silence on phone and TV.
- SponsorBlock: label sponsored videos, mark segments yourself, turn skips off per channel, and cut skipped parts out of
  downloads.
- Downloads carry their title, chapters, subtitles and cover inside the file, can take translated subtitles, and have search
  and Resume all.
- Save a screenshot, open a video in another player, set caption colour and place, and pick a quality preset.
- Each video can play again at the speed you last watched it at.
- History sorts and filters by day, week or month; History, Watch later and Liked have Select.
- Import from FreeTube, LibreTube and PipePipe, Google Takeout searches, and save or load your settings and download lists.
- Playlists save as a list of links or a CSV file, and videos move to the top or bottom.
- Your own proxy, Tor included, and voice search on the phone and the TV.
- Comments show their pictures, and Up next cards are tidier.

## Watch

- Subtitles show in the player on every device, in a look you set, and Sync automatically lines them up by listening.
- Skip intro, recap and credits for films and series, from the free intro databases or your Jellyfin, Emby or Plex server.
- A Calendar of new and coming episodes, and New episodes on Home.
- Mark films, episodes or whole seasons as watched; My list has a History chip.
- Torrents: open a magnet link or a .torrent file, play or download its files, share your own, and keep torrents on your VPN.
- Live TV: watch from the start or earlier in the day, pause and rewind live, a softly tinted guide, and Jellyfin or Emby
  Live TV.
- The player has Chapters, Playback info, Night and Clear voices, picture looks, seek pictures and sideways swipe to seek.
- Pick up to four languages for Home, each with its Top 10, films, web series and daily soaps.
- Play on a TV lists Chromecast and Google TV devices beside DLNA TVs.
- MDBList ratings, Simkl picks, season posters, episode ratings, notes, and Filler or Recap on anime episodes.
- Back up your Watch library to a file, choose a cache limit, and keep posters and subtitles with downloads.
- The next episode's copy is found before the current one ends, and stalled torrents pick up where they stopped.

## Read

### Books

- Opens Kindle (without DRM), FB2, Word, RTF, text, Markdown, saved web pages, CBZ comics and CHM help books.
- Highlight, take notes, export them, look words up (online or with your own dictionary) and read aloud.
- More type and page choices: your own fonts, spacing, justified text, two columns, Grey, Black and your own colours.
- Scroll by itself, speed reading, paragraph mode, a reading ruler, an e-ink mode and saved styles.
- Time left in the chapter and book, a foot line you choose, and your reading time by day.
- Sync through your own folder and KOReader, add whole book folders, and send your place to Komga and Kavita.
- PDFs read light on dark, reflow as text, zoom by column and take pen marks.
- Save a web page or pasted text as a book, and a highlight as a quote picture.
- Two books side by side on a wide screen, and a Reading now home screen widget.
- Send books to your other devices or LocalSend apps, and take books from calibre.
- Hardcover shows what you are reading and what you finished.
- Listen: audiobooks from this device, free LibriVox recordings and your own Audiobookshelf, with chapters, and books with
  their own narration read along.

### Manga

- Browse opens on a feed of your sources, with For you, top lists and Related series.
- The library, read chapters and places sync across your devices or a WebDAV folder.
- Restore Mihon backups and save one Mihon can restore; move a series to another source with its progress.
- Downloads wait in a queue you can pause, fetch new chapters, and can be saved as CBZ files.
- The reader crops borders, shows two pages side by side, splits double pages, turns top to bottom and plays moving pages.
- Colour filters, tap layouts, volume keys, an e-ink mode, and the direction and scale kept per series.
- Hide an image, such as an ad, wherever it comes back.
- More library filters and sorts, Incognito, duplicates merged, notes, bookmarks, statistics and genre and type filters.
- Updates group by series, show upcoming chapters and say which series could not be updated.
- MangaUpdates, Komga and Suwayomi stay up to date as you read.
- Manga can lock behind your screen lock, Windows Hello, Touch ID or a PIN.
- Novels: Wikisource and novel websites open in a text reader.
- A computer can share downloaded chapters with other readers as an OPDS catalogue.

## Podio

- Each followed show has its own settings: new episodes, skips at the start and end, speed, voice boost, downloads and tags.
- Bookmarks, favourites, lists that fill themselves, and Your listening time.
- Radio: add your own stations, record a station, recently played and Heard on the radio, genres and a radio alarm.
- Scrobble the songs stations play to Last.fm or ListenBrainz.
- Keep episodes whole as they play, so they go on without a connection.
- Private feeds with a user name and password, and a PIN lock for following new shows.
- Podcast search also looks in the gPodder and Podverse directories, and a podcast's page finds an episode by title.
- Skip sponsors in podcast episodes from YouTube, and comments from the fediverse when a feed links them.
- Sync carries your shows, stations and places to your other devices.
- More transcript formats and chapters.

## Desktop

- Sign in with your phone: scan a code and agree on the phone.
- Shortcuts work from any app on Windows, and the window title names the playing song.
- Windows gets its own minimise, maximise and close buttons, and the mode switcher opens a real menu.
- Floating lyrics above other windows from the tray menu or Ctrl+Shift+L.
- Play your WebDAV server or SMB share, and AIFF, DSD, Opus, APE and WavPack files.
- The tray and mini player follow and control a video playing in Video.
- The desktop stays awake while something plays, and radio reconnects on its own.
- Opening Cosmotune while it runs brings its window to the front.

## Phone

- A Lyrics widget, a Reading now widget and a Quick Settings tile that plays or pauses.
- Floating lyrics over other apps, and lyrics in the status bar.
- A sleep timer button on the notification.
- Long-press Cosmotune's icon for Rescue, which saves a backup when the app will not open.
- Downloads can go to a memory card, and files open with Cosmotune from your file manager.
- Music videos play as sharp as the screen shows, up to Full HD.
- Android Auto lists Podcasts and radio and your audiobooks.
- Held sideways, the side rail keeps every tab on screen; on a tablet the player keeps a phone's width.

## TV and watch

- Play from your phone sends a phone's video to Watch on the TV.
- A phone's video app can cast to Cosmotune with Link with TV code, or find it on the same Wi-Fi.
- The remote's colour, channel and wind buttons can be given player actions.
- The TV player shows the time while its card is up.
- The watch can save podcast episodes and play them with no phone.
- Listening on a speaker or the watch counts in Replay.

## Fixes

- A song whose upload is gone plays from another copy of the same song; one no source can play says so and moves on.
- Fixed music video matching: videos are looked for again when a song starts and reopened if they stop.
- Lyrics are looked up by the song's name rather than an upload's whole title.
- Songs match their uploads whatever the accents, and romanised titles are found.
- Music stays paused when an output comes or goes.
- Next on the notification never likes the song.
- Something removed from My list stays removed on every device.
- The desktop player's waveform no longer covers the artist name.
