# Cosmotune 1.0.6

## Changed

- Music's new-look Now Playing keeps its video in a wider **4:5 portrait frame** on Android, Windows, Linux and macOS. The crop stays fixed during playback; it does not pan after actors or switch to the full landscape video.
- On phones, the video sits above the expanded playback controls with room for the top bar and display cutout. Minimizing the controls does not resize the video. On desktop and tablets, the wider card keeps the lyrics/queue and transport clear. Phone landscape uses the same 4:5 card.
- Artist pages have taller motion/photo headers: 360 dp in the shared page and 400 dp on desktop, with a larger clear area before the desktop fade. Classic Android and desktop artist headers also have more height. Artist motion remains silent and follows the existing artwork, mobile-data and reduced-motion settings.

The frame change applies to Music's new-look player. Standalone Video mode, Shorts, the classic Music player and source video resolution keep their existing behaviour. A 4:5 crop still omits the sides of a landscape source; it cannot recover picture details absent from that source.

The remaining provider, Manga verification, casting and device/account limits from [1.0.4](../../docs/releases/1.0.4.md) still apply. See [pending work](../PENDING_WORK.md).

## Version

Public version 1.0.6; Android, Wear OS and TV upgrade code 11; desktop installer build 1.0.8.
