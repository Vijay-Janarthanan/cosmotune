# Cosmotune 1.0.36

Folders of your own videos with Reels, clearer playback errors, a programme guide for Live TV, a Continue shelf on Home and
one Settings screen for every mode.

## Highlights

- <!-- icon: playback --> **Clearer playback errors** — Each failure says what went wrong.
- <!-- icon: tv --> **Now and next** — The TV guide shows what's on, with times.
- <!-- icon: new --> **A Continue shelf** — Resume Watch, Podio, Read and music.
- <!-- icon: download --> **Folders and Reels** — Add a folder of videos and swipe through it.
- <!-- icon: settings --> **One Settings screen** — Every mode opens it at its own section.

## Every mode

- Home has a Continue shelf with the last things you watched, listened to or read, each with a Resume button.
- Settings in the profile menu opens the same Settings screen in every mode, with that mode's section already chosen. The
  other sections are one tap away, and Back returns you to where you were.
- On the phone, every tab shows its name, and the tab bar scrolls sideways when the text is large.
- Settings in the profile menu is called Settings in every mode, Video included.

## Music

- Music Home has a Show first choice: your personal mixes, music from your region, or the artists you follow.
- When a song's sources are compared, a source that fails now says why.
- Choose the video always lists the video that is playing, and finds the videos of songs whose name ends in a singer's tag.

## Video

- Local videos can add whole folders. A folder shows its folders and videos, each with a picture from the video, and
  the first video stands as the folder's picture.
- Reels plays a folder's videos, and those of every folder inside it, one at a time full screen, newest first. Swipe for the
  next one; on the desktop, the mouse wheel and the arrow keys do the same.
- Playing a video from a folder plays the ones after it too.
- Home gives more room to your subscriptions and what you watched recently.

## Watch

- When a film or channel will not play, Watch says why: the source timed out, offered no playable copy, uses a format this
  player can't play, the link expired, or the stream stopped buffering. Each comes with Retry or another source.
- Live TV shows what's on now and next, with local times, for channels whose add-on publishes a programme guide. Channels
  without a guide stay in a list below it and still play.
- Live TV search now lists every matching channel, not just the first eight.
- Search shows channels at once and each source's films and series as soon as that source answers, instead of waiting for
  the slowest one. The channel list no longer reloads from every source before a search after half an hour.
- Watch's settings, Plugins included, are now in the common Settings screen.

## Podio

- A station that drops says so, and Play reconnects it. An episode that stops says so, and Play picks it up where you were.

## Desktop

- Music that keeps playing while you browse Video mode keeps its player bar, so you can pause it; the bar steps aside while a
  video plays.
- Cosmotune hands memory it no longer needs back to the computer, instead of holding on to it for as long as it runs.
