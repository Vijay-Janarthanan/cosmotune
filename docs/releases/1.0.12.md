# Cosmotune 1.0.12

This release adds a video-feed look to Video mode, sign-in with a YouTube account, frame smoothing, picture-in-picture and a local video player, and repairs navigation out of Podio and several music-video search failures.

## Video mode

- The YouTube section now follows the layout of a video feed: a sticky chip row, one continuous feed of full-width cards (a grid that fills the window on desktop), channel pictures on every card, an inline Shorts row, Home/Shorts/Subscriptions/You tabs on a phone, and on desktop a search header with a collapsible guide sidebar. Watch pages have a channel row with a Subscribe pill, action chips and a full-card Up next list. Video-mode surfaces start dark.
- A channel's picture is taken from the item, from another item of the same channel or from the channel page, once per channel; a coloured initial shows until it arrives.
- Signing in with Google (the same sign-in as Music) lets Video mode read your Home recommendations, subscriptions feed, subscribed channels, playlists, Watch Later, Liked videos and History, and subscribe, like and save. It uses YouTube's web session as youtube.com does, which Google's terms do not permit for third-party apps and which YouTube can change or refuse at any time. Only the anonymous response shapes were checked against real responses; every signed-in read and write has not been run against a real account.
- Frame smoothing on desktop: Blend and Smooth (block motion estimation with a shader warp) up to the display rate or 48/60/90/120 fps, only below 30 fps by default, with Film and Anime presets and a measured "24 → 60 fps" readout that appears only while a conversion is really happening. It steps down to Blend and then Off when the machine cannot keep up. Smooth can look like the soap-opera effect on film. Android offers only a refresh-rate matching switch.
- Picture-in-picture: a borderless always-on-top window on desktop (drag, resize, remembered place, play/pause, ±10 s) and a manual button on the phone watch page.
- Local videos: open files from the top bar, the Library, Ctrl+O, drag and drop, or Android's Open with. The player remembers each file's position, has an aspect-ratio page (Fit, Fill, Stretch, 16:9, 4:3, 21:9, Original) and loads subtitle files found beside the video or picked.

## Fixes

- Podio and Manga on a phone have a Music button and answer the Back key; before, nothing on the page led out of them.
- Music-video search sends a stalled page request again on a fresh connection from its own request queue, and names every failed try in the log. Teasers shorter than half the song are rejected, an album named as a soundtrack is matched by its film, and when no publisher has the video a full-length upload by anyone is tried, with the audio check still deciding.
- Uncaught exceptions are written to `desktop.log`.

## Release limits

Watch Podio, bidirectional file synchronization and native DSD/DoP are not included. Smoothing, picture-in-picture, the local player, the new Video layout and the signed-in features were built and tested without a physical screen, device or Google account; platform builds and automated tests do not replace those checks. The OpenCanvas files changed here are recorded in [the import record](../OPENCANVAS_IMPORT.json).
