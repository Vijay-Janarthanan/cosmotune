# Cosmotune 1.0.32

A fix for the desktop app: switching a song's video between Whole and Fill no longer closes Cosmotune.

## Highlights

- <!-- icon: check --> **Fill no longer closes** — Whole and Fill switch without closing the app.

## Desktop

- Switching a playing video between Whole and Fill could close Cosmotune without a message. The video now changes size
  smoothly and keeps playing.

More in the [desktop guide](../../DESKTOP.md).
