# Cosmotune 1.0.16

This release adds shared rooms (listen, watch and talk together, each person in their own app) and an optional Cosmotune account that keeps your playlists, likes and followed artists the same on your devices. It also fixes lyrics and music videos freezing on the desktop when the window is not the focused one.

## Shared rooms

- Make a room, or join one with its six-character code, from Listen together (phone and desktop). Before you join you see who is inside. A room holds up to eight people (five by default).
- Whatever the host plays plays for everyone **in their own app**: nothing is streamed between devices. It can be a song, a video from Video mode, a live radio station or a podcast episode from Podio. Each person keeps their own quality, captions, speed and volume (a "Playback options" button opens them).
- "Play in room" (or "Watch together" / "Listen together" when you are not in a room) is on the Video watch page and on Podio stations and episodes. The shared queue, previous, next and seek work for everyone unless the host switches on host-only control.
- A room screen with Now Playing, Chat and People. Chat has song requests: anyone can ask for a song and anyone allowed to control the music can play it with one tap. The host can switch host-only control on and remove people.
- A room icon, with the number of people and an unread dot, is in the top bar or dock of every mode; one tap opens the room, and closing it returns you to the page you were on.
- **Voice** (first version): everyone joins muted and can unmute whenever they like, with no host permission. You can mute a person for yourself only, set their volume, or silence all voices on your device. Voice goes straight between devices; nothing passes through our servers. Android asks for the microphone at your first unmute, never when you join.
- Rooms need an internet connection and replace the old party-server screens. They use a guest account made on your device, or your Google account if you are signed in.

## Optional cloud account

- Settings > Account & sync has **Turn on cloud sync**, and **Sign in with Google** on Android and desktop. Until you turn it on, nothing is sent anywhere.
- It keeps playlists, likes and followed artists the same on your devices, merging by the newest change; a delete on one device is a delete everywhere. Audio, files on your device and your YouTube sign-in are never synced.
- **Sign out** stops syncing on that device. **Delete cloud account and data** removes everything stored for the account. The [privacy policy](https://cosmotune-vj-c4394.web.app/privacy.html) says what is stored and where.

## Fixes

- On Windows and macOS the lyrics and the music video stopped moving whenever you clicked another window. The app treated a window that lost focus as hidden. Only a minimised window is treated as hidden now. Android is unchanged.

## Release limits

- Rooms and voice have been tested against the real service with simulated devices, and the code is covered by automated tests, but they have **not** been tried by two people on real phones and computers, and nobody has listened to the voice yet. Expect rough edges and tell us what you find.
- Voice has no relay server. Two people on strict networks (many mobile connections) may not be able to connect to each other. If someone cannot be heard, check the microphone permission (on Windows, Settings > Privacy & security > Microphone > desktop apps).
- Voice echo: if you listen on speakers, your microphone also picks up the room's music and voices; headphones avoid it.
- Seeking inside Video's own player is not sent to the room (use the room's seek bar), and a live station has no position to seek.
- Google sign-in on a phone and the cloud sync on a second device have not been tried against real accounts yet. The new Android strings in languages other than English are machine-translated and need a native-speaker check.
