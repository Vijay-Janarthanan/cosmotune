# Cosmotune 1.0.5

A fix for the music video behind the song (OpenCanvas), found after 1.0.4.

## Fixed

- Songs from labels and channels without a verified badge now get a music video. The 1.0.3 search accepted only verified or official publishers and returned nothing for the rest; it now falls back to the best-ranked match, and the audio check still rejects a wrong one.
- The video appears sooner. Each YouTube search waits at most 4 seconds, and the search stops after 6 seconds once it has results.
- The video no longer switches between a close crop and the whole frame while playing. It fills the picture the same way on every song, as before 1.0.3. The subject-following crop stays in the code behind one switch (`OpenCanvas.SUBJECT_FRAMING`) and is off.

Everything else is as in [1.0.4](../../docs/releases/1.0.4.md).
