# Cosmotune 1.0.15

This release gives every mode one design, signs Music and Video in once with your YouTube account, and brings Manga, Read, Podio, Settings and search onto the same kit.

## One look across the app

- Music, Video, Podio, Read and Manga, Library and Settings now share one design system: neutral layered grounds, one set of radii and type sizes, pill buttons that keep their shape, flat buttons, two-line cards, and a dock that merges the mini player and the tab bar. Pure black is a toggle in Appearance, beside AMOLED and Material You.
- A mode switcher names the modes Music, Video, Podio, Read and Manga. Manga and Read are separate modes on the phone and the desktop.
- Music has four tabs (Home, Explore, Library, Releases) with Search as an icon in the top bar. Home, Library, album, artist and Now Playing are redrawn on the kit. Quality pills show the full resolution and a signal path.
- Video has a large-title Home with sticky chips, full-width cards and pull to refresh; Subscriptions with an avatar strip and four filter chips; a Watch page with one action row, a comments teaser and rows for up next; channel and playlist pages with a 16:5 banner, an overlapping avatar and a grid from 600 dp; and Shorts with plain icons on black and a rail with dislike. Video uses the shared top bar, dock mini player and account sheet.
- Podio's Radio, Podcasts, Episodes and Library pages, and Read's Home, Browse, Bookmarks and Library, are rebuilt on the kit. Settings has a hub with categories and a search that jumps to the row, with one card per section.

## Accounts and sources

- Music and Video share one YouTube session, with silent refresh. Signing in once serves both, from Music or in place in Video, and an expired sign-in shows one banner and one account sheet in both. Video shows your account's lists merged with local ones.
- Video plays, reads and writes with the signed-in session: comments with likes, replies and posting open in a sheet, and private playlists are available. These parts were tested against fake responses and have not been tried against a live account or a device.
- Search covers every provider package, with a "Search in" picker, automatic verification windows and per-source streaming with budgets that respect the queue. A provider session closes as soon as its request is cancelled.

## Fixes

- First-run setup no longer opens and vanishes on launch: it waits for the library, history and queue to load before deciding.
- Podio's show page says "1 episode" rather than "1 episodes".

## Release limits

Rendered screenshots of every main screen against the design references, and walkthroughs on a device with a signed-in account, are still to do. Some screens keep their old look: Replay cards, the desktop collection page header, the first-run "Your listening" dialog, and parts of the Manga, Read and Podio pages. Tamil and Telugu show Manga-only translations for the new mode names. Watch Podio, bidirectional file synchronization and native DSD/DoP are not included.
