# Cosmotune 1.0.19

## Highlights

- <!-- icon: read --> **More free books** — Tamil, Spanish and classic books you can keep offline.
- <!-- icon: watch --> **Watch tries again** — A film that failed starts once a working source appears.
- <!-- icon: tv --> **More on Android TV** — Video, Read, Manga and Podio arrive on the TV.
- <!-- icon: wear --> **Radio on your watch** — Play radio and podcasts on Wear OS without your phone.
- <!-- icon: podcast --> **Clear old episodes** — Remove downloaded episodes in Podio when you are done.

## Details

**Read → Browse** adds historical EPUBs and scans from Internet Archive, Spanish editions from textos.info, Tamil PDFs from Project Madurai, recent classics from Global Grey, and open textbooks from OpenStax. Supported EPUBs and PDFs open in Cosmotune and are available offline after download. Protected loans open with their provider. PDF Search on Android requires Android 15 or later.

**Watch and Live TV** can recover when a playable source arrives after an earlier failure. Retry uses the selected source. Watch saves the download index before showing a download as finished. The bundled torrent engine is enabled by default, and Spotlight remains reachable when its lists have no headline event.

**Android TV** adds Video, Read, Manga and Podio tools, with remote controls for the radio globe. **Wear OS** adds direct playback of public radio and podcasts over the watch’s internet connection. The paired phone mode is still available.

**Podio** can remove downloaded episodes after confirmation. **Replay** presents non-interactive chart rows as static content.

**Desktop audio previews** add native ALSA DSD on Linux and strict CoreAudio DoP for eligible macOS USB DACs. Both are opt-in and require an exact format. Automated checks cover framing and transport logic. Physical DAC, TV remote and watch playback validation is still outstanding. [Audio transport details](../DSD_TRANSPORT_AND_EQ.md)
