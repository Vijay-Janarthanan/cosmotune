# Cosmotune 1.0.14

The first build attempt caught a stale browser-helper version before publishing. The repaired 1.0.14 aligns all app/helper versions and checks them before platform builds. The failed tag was updated at the user's explicit request; no installers were published by that attempt.

This release brings books into **Read**, keeps Video playback controls reachable after leaving a video, and repairs provider/settings navigation across Android and desktop.

## Read: books and manga

- Open Books or Manga from one Read mode. Existing Manga libraries and reading progress stay in place.
- Import EPUB and PDF files for offline reading, resume where you stopped, save bookmarks and manage collections.
- Read supplied colour illustrations using native WebView/WebKit EPUB rendering and colour PDF pages. EPUB includes contents, chapter navigation, text search, typeface, size, line spacing, margins and page themes. PDF retains its original layout and provides page/zoom controls.
- Discover books through Open Library search and attributed trending, public-domain Gutenberg OPDS metadata, or a personal OPDS library. Save metadata separately from downloads and inspect edition-specific source links.
- Download supported full EPUB/PDF acquisitions offered by your configured source. Borrowing, samples and purchasing open with the provider. Commercial catalogue results do not imply free access.
- Manga discovery opens a usable catalogue, moves source management out of the reading flow and filters the requested `hent` source identities consistently.

## Playback and everyday controls

- A Video mini-player exposes pause, reopen and close after Back. Completed stream ranges can be reused during window restore within a bounded in-memory cache.
- Smoothing can advance with a fallback when analysis is late. Output cadence reporting includes gaps rather than inferring smooth output from a selected mode.
- Artist artwork retains a persistent fallback. Music/Video/Read/Podio profile and settings entry points behave consistently on phone and desktop.
- Provider sessions merge safely, preserve credentials on transient service failures and expose recoverable health states. Android receives the provider-management surfaces.
- Song lists gain search/sort and reachable row actions while preserving the chosen queue order. Video Home feeds and regional discovery recover from source failures.

## Listening, discovery and synchronization

- Save device-local EQ profiles, apply a flat bypass and inspect real output/spatial capabilities. Device guides cover Sony WH-1000XM5, Samsung earbuds and the Audiocular DAC without claiming control of unsupported OS codecs or headset ANC.
- Broader bounded local music discovery, explicit feedback and editable mix previews use the existing tiny local model; no always-on recommendation backend is required.
- Watch Podio actions use the paired phone as playback authority. File synchronization and playlist download updates add bounded manifests, journaling and conflict handling.
- Strict DoP source/output validation is available on the supported Windows/Linux desktop paths. Native DSD, Android USB DSD and macOS DoP are not advertised as implemented.

## Validation and limits

[Batch validation](../FEATURE_BATCH_VALIDATION_2026-11-02.md) records automated tests, native rendering/playback probes and their limits. Release assets publish only after the platform workflow passes.

EPUB search is per chapter. PDF text search is supported on desktop and Android 15+; older Android still reads PDFs. Password-protected/DRM books require their authorized reader. Paperback 0.9 extensions use a different runtime and are not executable in this release. Source availability, human verification and library borrowing depend on the provider. Physical DAC/headphone/watch checks and macOS/Linux interactive reader validation remain separate from compilation and fixture tests. The app cannot promise every commercial book free or every video perfectly smooth.
