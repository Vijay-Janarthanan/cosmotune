# Cosmotune 1.0.27

This update fixes signing in to cloud sync with Google on the phone, a crash from Watch's profile menu, and pages the new layouts
had left out of reach. Watch now shows every catalogue of your add-ons and keeps its place.

## Highlights

- <!-- icon: check --> **Google sign-in fixed** — Cloud sync signs in on the phone again.
- <!-- icon: settings --> **Phone cloud sync** — The account card opens it again.
- <!-- icon: look --> **Every add-on row** — Watch shows all of your catalogues.
- <!-- icon: check --> **Library right after sign-in** — Playlists show as soon as you sign in.

## Desktop

- Signing in again to the account you were signed in to before (after its sign-in ran out) now loads your library and
  playlists at once and takes the "Sign in to YouTube Music" card off Home. Before, Cosmotune waited for the account to change,
  which it does not when it is the same account, so the library stayed empty until a restart.
- For the first five minutes after you sign in, Cosmotune checks every half minute that YouTube still has the sign-in, and the
  log names any YouTube answer that clears it and the request it answered (cookie names only, never their values). A sign-in
  that YouTube ends early can then be traced instead of only being seen at the next start.

- The account card at the top of Settings opens the Account page (Account & integrations, cloud sync, and a new Switch
  account row) instead of the account switcher. Cloud sync could otherwise only be found by searching.
- From Podio, Manga, Read or Watch, the profile menu's Settings, Replay, Downloads and Equalizer, and Listen together, open in
  Music. Before, they did nothing until you went back to Music yourself.
- In a narrow window, Manga and Read show their own tabs, and History is in the profile menu. Before, the sidebar that holds
  them was hidden and there was no other way in.
- Each cloud sync now writes a line to the log with what it sent and received (counts only).

## Phone

- Sign in with Google under cloud sync works when your Google account already has Cosmotune data from another device. Before,
  it showed "Could not sign in with Google" and "Sync failed: BAD_RESPONSE: no user id", and the phone stayed a guest. It now
  signs in to that account and sends the phone's library to it.
- Settings or Sources & providers from Watch's profile menu no longer closes the app.
- Signed out of YouTube, the account card opens the Account page too, so cloud sync is in reach without signing in.
- The account card at the top of Settings opens the Account page again: cloud sync (Turn on cloud sync, Sign in with Google,
  Sync now) is there, with Account & integrations. Since the new Settings layout it opened Account & integrations directly, and
  cloud sync could only be found by searching. With cloud sync on and the same Google account on both devices, your playlists,
  likes, followed artists and Watch add-ons reach the desktop, and Continue from your phone works.
- The account card shows your saved profile's name and picture while YouTube's account details are loading or could not be
  read, instead of a blank person.
- Each device shows the YouTube channel it listens as (Listen as): a brand channel on one and your own on the other show
  different names. Choose the same one under Listen as on both to match them.

## Watch (phone and desktop)

- Watch's Home shows a row for every catalogue of every add-on you have on, not only the first four of each: the first ones
  come with the page and the rest fill in as you scroll to them.
- Coming back from a film or series finds Home where you left it, with the titles a row had loaded still there.
- A new or removed add-on shows on Home and in Live TV the next time you open them, instead of after up to 15 minutes.
