# Cosmotune 1.0.4

This release adds a Wear OS companion, an Android TV app, a browser player with a helper extension, Manga mode, provider packages, a set of library tools and a phone remote for the desktop and the TV. The [feature tracker](../../FEATURE_TRACKER.md) has the row-by-row detail and the tests behind each item; "Known limits" below lists what is not finished or not yet tried on real devices.

## What is new

### Music on the phone and the desktop

- Artist pages show the artist's photo and, where Apple Music has one, a short silent moving header. It follows the animated artwork, reduced motion and mobile data settings.
- Optional artist signatures from a signature service you set up yourself under Settings. There is no default service.
- The new player shows the audio format that is actually being decoded (codec, sample rate, bit depth, bitrate).
- The queue can show recently played songs above Now playing (a setting on both platforms).
- The equalizer has a limiter under it, so boosting bands no longer clips. On Android, dragging one band no longer moves the others back.
- Desktop Automix: choose 8 or 16 bar phrases and "Automix on skip" in settings; the transition effects were fixed (stereo channels no longer bleed into each other).
- Search has a third source, Add-ons: your enabled HTTP add-ons and provider packages are searched together when you press Enter. Rows show the quality the add-on offers.
- Only the song that is playing is highlighted in a list, not every song with the same name.
- Shelves and carousels stop on a card instead of between two.
- Lyrics in Hebrew, Arabic and other right-to-left scripts sit on the correct side in the new look, whatever the app's language.
- Lyrics can be saved as an `.lrc` file from the share menu; a `.lrc` file next to a local song is read.
- Android: an option to hide the status bar in the player; Replay has a "This week" view; the Like button on the widget and notification appears only when it can work (signed in, or a source that supports it); Spotify sign-in opens over the app and offers "Use sp_dc token".
- Desktop: a small always-on-top mini player (tray menu, or Ctrl/Command+Shift+M); the Windows media card's scrubber seeks; full screen uses a borderless window over the taskbar; your own playlists have "Edit order"; a build without a Last.fm key asks for your own API key; the account button shows the selected YouTube profile's picture and name; Replay can save a share card as a PNG.
- Linux: the MPRIS media controls seek correctly (a 5-second skip no longer jumps to 0:05) and report the live position.
- Radio can take suggestions from your Last.fm account (similar tracks, your top artists). Off by default; turn it on under the Last.fm settings.
- A small private learner adjusts recommendations to your plays and skips, on the device. It can be switched off and reset.
- ListenBrainz listens go through the same offline queue as Last.fm scrobbles, so they are sent later when you are offline.
- Casting: songs the Chromecast receiver cannot play (DSD, AIFF, APE, WavPack, ALAC, FLAC above 96 kHz) are refused with a message instead of a silent receiver. When casting ends, the song continues on the phone at the same second, and plays only if you chose this phone.
- Android 14 and newer: an optional "Bit-perfect USB output" setting asks Android for its bit-perfect USB mode and shows whether it was granted. Both platforms now say "Bit-perfect" only when that is confirmed, and otherwise name what changes the sound.

### Library tools

Under Listening tools, on the phone and the desktop:

- Analyse local audio: loudness (EBU R128, ReplayGain 2.0 track and album gain), peak and a spectral check. Normalization uses the measured values for local files.
- Duplicate review; byte-identical copies can be merged, with the extra copy moved to the system trash if you choose.
- Tag editing for FLAC, MP3, Ogg Vorbis/Opus and MP4/M4A files.
- M3U8 playlist export.
- Quality review and a preview of lossless upgrades: a saved lossy song can point at an analysed lossless copy of the same recording. "Get lossless copies from sources" queues lossless downloads. Audio files are never overwritten or deleted.
- Library columns (album, length, source, format, quality, loudness, genre) and filters by source, quality and genre in the local and downloaded song lists.
- One-artist names you add are respected when matching songs, and local files without artist tags fall back to the album artist, the file name or the folder.
- Linked playlists: link a YouTube Music playlist, preview its changes, keep removed songs as placeholders that come back when they return.
- Missing downloads are repaired at start-up when the file is found under the same name; the rest show as failed with Retry.
- A release inbox for followed artists, with suggestions from your library and an optional daily check while the app is open.
- More smart playlist rules.
- Downloads keep their queue across restarts, pace each provider separately, and a failed desktop transfer is shown as failed with Retry instead of staying "Running".
- A song is labelled lossless only when its bytes are lossless: a download that claims FLAC but delivers AAC is saved and shown as AAC.

### Wear OS

- A watch app for the phone: now playing with a progress ring, play/pause, hold to seek, skip, Love, shuffle, repeat, Up Next, phone volume on the crown or bezel, and an always-on player.
- The watch can be the speaker: the phone keeps the queue and sends the audio to the watch, so the watch needs no network or account. Use it with the watch speaker or Bluetooth headphones.
- The phone installs and updates the watch app (Settings, Cosmotune for Wear OS), checking the file before it is sent.

### Android TV

- A separate TV app with a D-pad interface, profiles with PINs, USB music, setup and display options, and sign-in on the TV.
- Wrong PINs now count toward a cooldown that also holds across a restart.
- Settings, Connections, Phone remote opens a paired controller in your phone's browser.
- The TV app updates itself only with TV packages for its own processor type.

### Browser companion

- A browser player (search, album/artist/playlist pages, your account libraries, queue and radio, history, timed lyrics, video and motion artwork, Spotify library and Canvas, Listen Together) with a helper extension for Chromium browsers and Firefox.
- The helper keeps account cookies and tokens inside the extension and only answers the site addresses it was built for. Discord stays off.
- Nothing is hosted for you: the release has the site as a zip to host yourself, and the two helpers as unpacked/unsigned builds.

### Manga

- A third mode beside Music and Video. Music keeps playing while you read; a video is stopped when you switch to Manga.
- Read CBZ/ZIP files and image folders, with chapters taken from subfolders and details from `ComicInfo.xml`.
- Online sources through the Kotatsu parsers, search across all enabled sources, series pages with language/edition filters, unread counts and new chapters after "Check for updates".
- Right-to-left, left-to-right and webtoon reading, zoom with buttons, keys and pinch, fit page or fit width.
- Offline chapter downloads, reading history and progress.
- Your own website sources from HTML or JSON templates, and an optional Komga server.
- Android only: Mihon/Keiyoushi extension APKs, reviewed before anything in them runs.

### Providers and add-ons

- Provider packages (`.sflx`, Mornye/SpotiFLAC) with repositories over HTTPS, a review of identity, hash and permissions before install, settings, updates you approve, and Home sections from enabled providers.
- Packages that only download to a file can now play.
- HTTP add-ons: settings declared by the add-on are editable and saved, album and artist pages open, a refresh re-reads the add-on's description, and add-on answers are limited to 2 MiB.
- Subsonic and OpenSubsonic servers (Navidrome, Gonic and others) as a music source on both platforms.

### Remote

- Listening tools, Phone remote on the desktop (and Settings on the TV) shows a QR code. Scan it with a phone on the same network to control playback, seek, volume, shuffle, repeat, Love, Up Next and to search for music. Each start makes a new pairing key; Stop disconnects every phone.

## Security fixes

- Android now keeps six provider credentials out of cloud backups: the Last.fm session key, API key and shared secret, the ListenBrainz token, the Spotify sp_dc cookie and the PaxSenix key are stored in the app's encrypted store, which backups exclude. Copies in the old plain settings are moved on first start.
- The desktop keeps the Last.fm session key, API key and secret, the ListenBrainz token and the Discord token, and, where they were still in plain preferences, the YouTube Music cookie fallback and the source list's secrets, in Windows DPAPI (current user) on Windows, and in owner-only files (folder 0700, file 0600) in the app data folder on macOS and Linux. Old plain values are moved and deleted on first read; if DPAPI fails, nothing is written in plain text.
- The watch app file is checked against its SHA-256 before it is sent from the phone; a damaged copy is deleted.
- Add-on and provider answers are size-limited before they are read into memory.

## Known limits

Not finished in this release:

- Album and playlist playback on Android now goes through the bounded queue, but it has only been tested on a computer, not yet on a phone.
- The desktop's Windows media-card seeking and borderless full screen were ported from upstream by reading; they are compiled for the first time by the Windows build on GitHub and have not been tried on a Windows PC yet. The upstream right-click song menu on the desktop's own rows is not included.
- Provider packages that stream HLS or DASH, or that need FFmpeg, do not work yet.
- Manga websites that require a browser check (a "verify you are human" page) cannot be used yet; Cosmotune tells you to open the website.
- Party server changes (token in the WebSocket protocol, tuning) need a server update and have not been tested on a server yet.

Decided against, for now:

- No alternative app icons and no new welcome screens.
- Discord stays off.
- No exclusive or hog-mode output on the desktop, no native DSD or DoP; DSD is converted to PCM.
- No concert listings, music haptics or desktop widgets.
- No "Lite" build.
- No per-video sync offset for music videos.
- No custom Chromecast receiver, no casting from the desktop, and the TV app is not a Cast target.
- No Soulseek.

Other limits:

- Video playback on macOS has still not been verified; the music side works, but Video mode may not start.
- The watch cannot play songs the phone serves as a DASH stream. Installing the watch app from the phone needs Android 11 or newer on the phone.
- Manga's interface is in English only. CBR, CB7 and PDF files are not supported. Mihon extensions work on Android only. On Android a Komga server must use https.
- Subsonic servers show no cover art (cover addresses carry your password). On macOS and Linux the desktop keeps credentials in owner-only files, not in the system keychain.
- The Add-ons search labels are English in every language.
- New text in the 18 languages was translated without review by native speakers. Corrections are welcome.

Many features need hardware or accounts that were not available while testing, so they have been checked by automated tests but not on real devices: a Wear OS watch (pairing, the crown and bezel, audio on the watch), an Android TV with a remote, a USB DAC, a Chromecast, Windows and macOS media controls and full screen, Linux media panels, a real Subsonic or Komga server, a party server, and signed-in Spotify, Last.fm and ListenBrainz accounts. The [device checklist](../DEVICE_CHECKLIST_1.0.4.md) lists these checks.

## Version

The app version is 1.0.4. The Android, Wear OS and TV upgrade code advances to 9 (1.0.3 was 7); the desktop numeric installer build advances to 1.0.7, so Windows and macOS installers upgrade an existing installation in place.
