# Cosmotune 1.0.35

The player's seek line is the plain line with its round thumb again.

## Highlights

- <!-- icon: playback --> **The plain seek line is back** — A thin line with a round thumb, as before 1.0.34.

## Music

- The waveform seek line is now off by default. Turn it on in Settings, Player, Waveform seek line.
- With it on, the line stays plain until the song's shape is learned, instead of showing a row of dots.
- The waveform keeps a round thumb where the song is, and comment marks sit on the line rather than under it.
