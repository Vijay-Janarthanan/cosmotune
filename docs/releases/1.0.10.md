# Cosmotune 1.0.10

This release fixes music videos that were never found or that replayed footage, shows the film a song is from, and gives the desktop app a tray player and taskbar controls.

## Music videos

- A song whose video file came back with an unreadable frame table (the progressive MP4 for "Chinna Thamarai" had a table of numbers that are not sizes, and FFmpeg refuses it too) was called silent, so its official video was never found. That file now counts as unreadable and the song and video are measured again through the other kind of stream, which finds the video.
- The picture no longer goes back to footage it has already shown. A short step back in the measured map is a wrong match and the previous offset carries on (for "Thaen Thaen Thaen" the video had jumped back 2.5 s and 3.1 s); a longer step back leaves the cover showing until the video moves past what was shown.
- A video search waits up to 8 seconds again. Versions 1.0.5 to 1.0.9 cut it to 4 seconds, which dropped slow searches and left songs with no video. A search that gets no answer is now written to the log.
- On the desktop, a song played from the bar looks up its film or album before the video search, as a song played from the open player always did.

## Provider sources

- A song whose artist is spelled with different word breaks in two catalogues was refused as a different artist: YouTube Music says "Yuvanshankar Raja" and Amazon Music says "Yuvan Shankar Raja", so "Machaan Machaan" showed "Amazon Music has no copy" although Amazon lists it, and Amazon was never asked for the audio. The same name with different word breaks now counts as the same artist (a short run of letters or a different artist that shares one word still does not).
- A row from a provider package whose service cannot serve it no longer leaves the song silent. The YouTube Music package answers "Provider could not resolve this recording (api_error)" for every recording, so a song picked from its shelves (such as "Veezha Veera") did not play at all. The song is now looked up by title and artist on the other sources, such as Amazon Music, YouTube and JioSaavn, with the failed package left out.
- A song that cannot be played, and a source page that cannot be loaded, now write the reason to the log; before, the screen said so and the log did not.

## Player

- The film or album name is shown under the artist in the player, and in the caption of the wide player.
- The audio line of the player now starts with a pill naming the tier of what is actually playing, read from the decoder: Hi-Res Lossless (lossless past 16-bit or 48 kHz), Lossless, High quality (lossy at 256 kbps or more) or Dolby Atmos.

## Desktop tray, taskbar and Dock

- Left click on the tray icon opens a card with the cover as its blurred background, the title and artist, progress, and previous, play or pause and next. A double click still opens the window.
- Right click opens the menu in the app's own style instead of the system's: what is playing, then Play or Pause, Next, Previous, Open, Mini player, Settings and Quit.
- Both open beside the icon on any screen edge, and close when you click away or press Escape. This is for Windows and macOS; Linux keeps its own tray menu.
- On Windows the taskbar button has previous, play or pause and next under its hover preview, and while a song is loaded the preview is a card with the cover and the song's name instead of the window. These belong to the taskbar button, so they exist while the window is open or minimized, not while it is closed to the tray. Setting `COSMOTUNE_TASKBAR=off` in the environment turns them off.
- On macOS the Dock menu has Play or Pause, Next and Previous, and the Dock icon is the cover while a song is loaded and the app's own icon again when nothing plays.

## Known limits

- The tray, taskbar and Dock features were checked against the real Windows shell (the buttons and the preview card are accepted, button clicks arrive) and by rendering the cards, not by watching a physical taskbar hover or a tray click. The macOS parts were not run on a Mac.
- A song with no official video that lines up with it still shows its cover.
- The Amazon Music fix was checked against the live Amazon catalogue up to the audio request. The audio itself needs a verified provider session, which the test profile did not have.
- The three OpenCanvas files changed here (`MusicVideoSearch.kt`, `Mp4FrameSizes.kt`, `CanvasSession.kt`) are recorded in [the import record](../OPENCANVAS_IMPORT.json) and are to be applied in the OpenCanvas repository.
