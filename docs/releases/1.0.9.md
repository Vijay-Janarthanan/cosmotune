# Cosmotune 1.0.9

This release rebuilds Podio's radio page around a globe with satellite imagery, and adds a page for the station that is playing.

## Radio map

- The globe shows NASA Blue Marble satellite imagery (public domain, no account) and zooms down to city level with the wheel, a pinch, double tap or the buttons. A ring in the middle picks the nearest dot, plus any dots crowded round it, so two stations close together can both be chosen. A button jumps to a random place.
- A station with no coordinates in the directory is drawn in its city or state when its name or region says so (Chennai, Coimbatore, Madurai, Arani, Vellore, Tamil Nadu and dozens more places in India), else where its language is spoken, else near its country's capital. Such stations are marked as approximate.
- Up to 3,000 stations are fetched for a search (it was 1,000), so every Indian station in the open directory is included.
- Arani Saaral FM is added from the station's own website; a bundled list (`podio/extra-stations.json`) holds stations whose own page publishes the stream but which the directory does not list.

## Radio page

- The desktop page is one panel beside the map, with a row of search and filters above them and nothing laid over anything else. The panel has Explore (what is under the ring), Favorites, Browse (regions and languages), Search (the full result) and Settings tabs.
- On a phone the globe fills the page with a search pill and a filter button on top, and one small stack at the bottom: the place, a one-row player and the tabs. Tapping the place opens its stations. Podcasts is a tab.
- The station that is on shows its name, place and the song on air when the station sends it, with previous, stop/play, next, the heart and, on the desktop, a volume slider. A station with no logo gets a tile in its own colour with its initials.
- The desktop sidebar of Podio and Manga now has rows like Music's and Video's (Radio, Podcasts, Favourite stations, Following; Library, Discover). The "Music" button on the page is gone: the mode is chosen from the profile menu. The bottom bar for a live station shows play and LIVE only: no previous, next, shuffle, repeat, length or lyrics.

## Station page

Clicking the bottom bar (or the player card) while a station plays opens its page: the map held on its place, the time there, the weather (temperature, sunrise and sunset, wind), photographs taken near it with their authors and licences, short articles about the area, and the other stations of the same place.

## Podcasts

The Podcasts tab opens on the top chart for the region, with genre chips (News, Comedy, Technology and others), as a grid of covers. A language filter searches shows in that language.

## What it contacts

New in this release, asked for only when the map or a station page is open: NASA GIBS for map tiles (gibs.earthdata.nasa.gov), Open-Meteo for the weather, and Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons for articles and photographs. The README's table lists them and [NOTICE](../../NOTICE.md) gives the credits and licences (Open-Meteo CC BY 4.0; Wikipedia text CC BY-SA 4.0; each photograph under its author's licence).

## Known limits

- The new radio pages were checked by rendering them and by the test suite, not on a physical phone or a running desktop window.
- Radio coverage is the open directory's: it has no station for Vellore town or Gudiyatham, for example. Stations can be added to Radio Browser (radio-browser.info) and appear here without an update.
- Imagery, weather, articles and photographs need an internet connection; without one the globe falls back to the drawn coastlines and the station page shows less.
- Local time and weather come from the place's coordinates; a station drawn at an approximate place shows that place's time, which is the same zone for any one country except the largest.
