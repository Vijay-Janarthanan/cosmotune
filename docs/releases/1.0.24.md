# Cosmotune 1.0.24

This update brings the reviewed upstream improvements into Cosmotune's existing Orbit interface on Android and desktop.

## Highlights

- <!-- icon: playback --> **Music start choices** — Start fast or wait for the best quality.
- <!-- icon: settings --> **Player options** — Keep song speed, pitch and lyric preferences.
- <!-- icon: download --> **Download your way** — Choose quality or queue a season of episodes.
- <!-- icon: new --> **More from podcasts** — Open chapters, transcripts and chapter artwork.
- <!-- icon: check --> **Recoverable sources** — Retry providers and roll back parser updates.

## Music and providers

- Choose Fast start, Best quality first, or No automatic upgrades. Replacement streams must pass recording and decoder checks; a failed replacement keeps the current stream playing.
- Prefer original, non-DRC YouTube audio when available. Substitute masters no longer inherit an unrelated YouTube loudness adjustment. Language and edition checks reduce wrong-recording matches.
- Provider packages recover from temporary startup/network failures with bounded backoff and a Try again action, preserving enabled choices and credentials.
- Updated torrent-engine dependencies and compiler; Firebase development tooling passes a clean dependency audit and emulator tests.
- Artist pages and followed artwork retain exact catalogue identity. Failed image refreshes keep the saved portrait. Local-file ratings survive rescans; desktop indexing reuses unchanged metadata.
- Provider download choices use the package's actual quality options, with independent progress, cancellation and retry per quality. Portable playlist imports retain unresolved rows for review.
- Player options add per-song speed/pitch, inline lyric alignment and opt-in playback behavior without replacing the Orbit design. Existing duet timing and the excluded lyric modes remain unchanged.

## Watch, Podio and Manga

- Watch supports persistent serial episode-download batches, including season, unwatched and custom choices. Restarted jobs resolve fresh streams. Downloads currently require direct MP4/MKV/WebM files; this does not add adaptive-stream or torrent downloading.
- Watch layouts adapt to short landscape windows and tablets; add-on metadata, episode IDs and inline headers retain their full identity. Library completion changes request synchronization without continuous polling.
- Podio exposes publisher-provided chapters and timed transcripts. Chapter artwork follows playback in system media metadata, without replacing the episode in the queue. A first follow refreshes the inbox.
- Manga uses the reviewed Kotatsu-Redo source revision with signed, platform-specific parser updates. The app verifies its pinned public key, runtime contract, hashes and bounds before loading code, and retains rollback/replay protection. Source exclusions remain enforced.

## System playback

- macOS gains native Now Playing artwork and media commands through an original MediaPlayer bridge.
- Music Cast and DLNA handovers guard receiver identity and STOP acknowledgement before starting a second local/remote output.

## Validation and limits

See the [validation record](../UPSTREAM_VALIDATION_2026-11-16.md), [review index](../UPSTREAM_REVIEW_2026-11-16.md) and linked per-area implementation records for test evidence. Platform installers are published only after their Release workflow gates pass.

Physical DAC/Bluetooth formats, audible cross-provider handovers, real Cast receivers and Android downloaded-parser execution require device checks. The update does not claim new direct USB transport, JOC object rendering, DRM access or support for every upstream platform-specific feature.
