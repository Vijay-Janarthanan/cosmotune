# Cosmotune 1.0.33

Pick your own cover for the playlists you made, Repeat on the phone's notification, and lyrics that keep the sung line in
view.

## Highlights

- <!-- icon: look --> **Your own covers** — Pick a picture for playlists you made.
- <!-- icon: playback --> **Repeat on the notification** — Repeat now sits next to Like.
- <!-- icon: lyrics --> **Lyrics stay in view** — The sung line no longer slips off screen.
- <!-- icon: lyrics --> **Letters rise in turn** — Sung words lift one letter at a time.
- <!-- icon: check --> **Pause on mute resumes** — Turn the volume up and the song plays on.

## Library

- Your own playlists get "Change cover" under Rename in their ⋯ menu, and a tap on the round cover opens the same picker.
  Pick one of the playlist's song covers, or choose a photo and place it in the circle. The page takes its colour from the
  new picture. "Use the automatic cover" puts the usual one back.
- The cover is kept on the device, and in your Cosmotune account when you are signed in. YouTube Music never gets it.

## Now Playing and lyrics

- When lyrics from another source replaced the ones showing while a song played, the list could fall behind until the
  line being sung was off the screen. It now follows the new lyrics.
- In lyrics with word timings, the letters of the word being sung rise one after another. Tamil, Hindi, Arabic and other
  scripts whose letters join still lift the whole word.
- Writers' credits at the top of a lyrics file are no longer shown as sung lines.
- A band whose name has a comma or an ampersand stays one artist in scrobbles and in the artists under a song.

## Playback

- The phone's notification and lock screen show Like and Repeat. Repeat goes off, all, one, as in the app. When a song
  can't be liked, Shuffle takes Like's place. Cars keep their buttons as before.
- With Pause on mute on, turning the volume back up plays the song on, unless you played or paused it yourself meanwhile.
- An album that YouTube Music lists by its music videos plays as its songs.

## Desktop

- Playlist covers work on the desktop too, with a file chooser for photos.

## Manga

- The Manga sources are updated, with fixes for several sites and two new ones. One adult source that was not labelled as
  adult is now left out.
