# Cosmotune 1.0.20

## Highlights

- <!-- icon: search --> **Pick the best copy** — Filter a film's sources by quality and see each file's size.
- <!-- icon: playback --> **Cleaner full screen** — Tap the picture to hide the controls, and skip 30 seconds.
- <!-- icon: watch --> **Another source to try** — If a film will not play, Watch offers other sources.
- <!-- icon: read --> **About every title** — Plot, rating and cast on every film and series page.

## Details

- Watch source lists filter by resolution and provider while keeping the original ranking. Stream rows show file size and duration when supplied, and distinguish a film’s catalogue runtime from the runtime of a particular copy.
- The Watch and Video fullscreen players hide the controls when you tap the picture. Their transport controls skip 30 seconds in either direction, and open menus stay visible while you use them.
- Watch offers alternative sources when playback fails. On Android TV, source lists scroll, and the remote’s Left and Right buttons skip 30 seconds when the controls are hidden.
- Movie and series pages show an About section with the full plot, rating, credits, cast and related titles when available. The regular Watch player shows the same details below its controls, including the current episode.
- Watch keeps the plot and rating from the catalogue card when the detail response omits them. Film sources remain available if the full details fail to load, and series retain season and episode navigation.
