# Cosmotune 1.0.29

This update finds the video of more Tamil and other Indian-language songs, prefers real video songs over still posters, and
keeps the desktop app open when a video frame cannot be drawn.

## Highlights

- <!-- icon: look --> **More song videos** — Spellings like Kathal and Kadhal now match.
- <!-- icon: check --> **Video songs first** — A "Video Song" beats a plain upload.
- <!-- icon: settings --> **No more closing** — A bad video frame is skipped.

## Song videos (phone and desktop)

- A song's video is found when its name is spelled differently in English letters: a song tagged "Kathal Oviyam" from "Alaigal
  Oyvatillai" sung by "Jensy" now matches its videos titled "Kadhal Oviyam … Alaigal Oivathillai … Jency". Before, YouTube's
  search found them and Cosmotune left every one out. Every other check stays: a verified label or the artist's own channel,
  the length, and the audio lining up with the song.
- When a label's or the artist's own upload of a song only says "Song" (often a still poster with the song as its sound), an
  upload that says it is the video song ("Video Song", "HD VideoSong") and names the song with its artist or film is tried
  first. "VideoSong" written as one word now counts as saying so.
- The log shows the picture measured for every candidate video, so a still picture that is taken for a video can be seen.

## Desktop

- A video frame the PC is too short of memory to draw is skipped, with the cover showing behind it, instead of closing the
  window in the middle of a song.
