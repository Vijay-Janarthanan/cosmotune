# Mornye: lossless, home feed and feature review

Reviewed 2026-10-23 from public source only. This is a report and proposed roadmap, not a claim that Mornye providers or features have been integrated into Cosmotune.

Inspected [Mornye](https://github.com/mornyeapp/Mornye) at `fb4945be910b0a7a8cce25eab3d1b3d80b3541e4` and [Mornye-Extension](https://github.com/mornyeapp/Mornye-Extension) at `6b544663fb5b7a7dfbe1959f3c0edab017378f53`. No external application/extension code was executed, no account was signed in, and no lossless audio payload was downloaded for this review. Provider availability, audible quality and physical DAC behaviour remain untested.

## Findings that matter for Cosmotune

Mornye separates **catalogue metadata, home discovery, audio resolution and output**. These are independent capabilities: a service may supply album/artist information without supplying lossless audio. Installing a metadata extension cannot turn a YouTube stream into FLAC. Converting lossy audio into a FLAC container does not recover missing audio information.

Its current architecture is Flutter UI with a native **Rust workspace running on the device**, including a QuickJS extension runtime. The directory name `rust_backend` does not mean every feature requires a hosted backend. However, some audio extensions depend on an external gateway, so it is also incorrect to call the whole source ecosystem server-independent. The [workspace](https://github.com/mornyeapp/Mornye/blob/fb4945be910b0a7a8cce25eab3d1b3d80b3541e4/rust_backend/Cargo.toml) and [extension runtime](https://github.com/mornyeapp/Mornye/blob/fb4945be910b0a7a8cce25eab3d1b3d80b3541e4/rust_backend/crates/extensions/src/runtime.rs) show this separation.

Cosmotune already has native Android and desktop decoders, source resolution, DSP and a tiny ranker. Importing the entire Flutter/Rust application would introduce another UI/runtime and packaging pipeline. Prefer adapting specific concepts or provider contracts after proving a benefit and measuring package cost.

## How the attractive home feed works

The [Explore provider](https://github.com/mornyeapp/Mornye/blob/fb4945be910b0a7a8cce25eab3d1b3d80b3541e4/lib/providers/explore_provider.dart) selects an enabled extension advertising `homeFeed`, asks it for sections and normalizes the results. It caches fresh content for five minutes, keys by provider/region, retains content during refresh and discards obsolete requests. This is useful product engineering, independent of a large recommendation model.

For a concrete example, the source-backed [YouTube Music extension](https://github.com/mornyeapp/Mornye-Extension/blob/6b544663fb5b7a7dfbe1959f3c0edab017378f53/sources/ytmusic-spotiflac/index.js) implements `getHomeFeed` by requesting `FEmusic_home` with a configured country and returning up to ten parsed sections. That inspected request does not establish Spotify-style account personalization. Some other providers are package-only in this checkout, so their internal behaviour was not inferred from registry descriptions.

**Recommendation:** retain Cosmotune's YouTube home support and add a provider-independent feed contract where needed: region/language, sections, source-qualified IDs, pagination, refresh, short cache TTL, partial failures and deduplication. Blend continue-listening, recent discoveries, followed artists and regional releases with local ranking. Show the origin of a feed rather than implying that every shelf was generated by our model.

## Their small on-device learning model

There is also real local personalization, separate from the home feed. [autoplay_personalization.dart](https://github.com/mornyeapp/Mornye/blob/fb4945be910b0a7a8cce25eab3d1b3d80b3541e4/lib/services/autoplay_personalization.dart) implements online logistic regression with AdaGrad over 4,096 hashed features. Two Float64 arrays occupy **65,536 bytes of numeric state**; Base64/JSON persistence and runtime objects add overhead.

It learns artist, song, album, genre, duration, time-of-day and previous-track interactions. It counts audible elapsed listening rather than treating a seek or paused time as engagement, downweights old learning and limits the adjustment while observations are scarce. [Autoplay](https://github.com/mornyeapp/Mornye/blob/fb4945be910b0a7a8cce25eab3d1b3d80b3541e4/lib/services/music_player_autoplay.dart) ranks bounded local-library candidates off the UI thread, rejects stale queue results and falls back to ordinary ranking.

**Recommendation:** a small online preference layer is a good experiment on top of Cosmotune's current quantized retrieval/ranking model. It does not replace the discovery catalogue or guarantee better recommendations. Preserve our under-5-MB constraint, evaluate skip/completion/diversity on held-out sessions, provide reset/opt-out, and measure p50/p95 on a phone before adopting it. No monthly full-model training job is necessary for this local preference layer; catalogue refreshes and personal learning are separate.

## Where lossless comes from

The [extension registry](https://github.com/mornyeapp/Mornye-Extension/blob/6b544663fb5b7a7dfbe1959f3c0edab017378f53/registry.json) lists metadata, lyrics and download providers with different capabilities. A listed service name is not proof that its audio route currently works or is officially supported by that service.

The inspected [Qobuz manifest](https://github.com/mornyeapp/Mornye-Extension/blob/6b544663fb5b7a7dfbe1959f3c0edab017378f53/sources/qobuz-web/manifest.json) advertises standard and hi-res lossless tiers but requires `signedSession@3`, `sessionGrant@1` and `preparedContext@1`, with a gateway at `api.zarz.moe`. The [Amazon manifest](https://github.com/mornyeapp/Mornye-Extension/blob/6b544663fb5b7a7dfbe1959f3c0edab017378f53/sources/amazon/manifest.json) also declares a signed-session gateway. These are dependencies to investigate with the operator; they are not reusable anonymous official audio APIs. This review did not attempt to impersonate the Mornye app to obtain a session.

Mornye's `.sflx` JavaScript packages are **not compatible** with Cosmotune's HTTP JSON add-on manifest just because both say “extension.” They expect host APIs for fetch, permissions, storage/files, session grants, downloads and sometimes media preparation. Supporting them would require a deliberate runtime/adapter implementation with resource limits, lifecycle cancellation, protected credentials and per-provider validation.

For Cosmotune, the lower-cost path is the existing JSON add-on protocol or direct authenticated/user-owned sources. Search metadata → match the recording/edition using IDs, title/artist and duration → resolve a playable stream → decode → report the actual input. Preserve request headers, expiry, seeking/range support and clear fallback reasons. Treat source claims separately from observed codec, sample rate, bit depth and encoded bitrate. Never display PCM throughput as a compressed file's bitrate.

The user's separately supplied Qobuz JSON add-on was queried for manifest, search and stream **metadata only**. It declared FLAC/24-bit/44.1 kHz for one test track at both requested quality tiers and did not return encoded bitrate. Its audio bytes were not verified. The private access URL is deliberately excluded from this report and must not be embedded in the application.

## Other feature opportunities

The [project feature list](https://github.com/mornyeapp/Mornye/blob/fb4945be910b0a7a8cce25eab3d1b3d80b3541e4/README.md) describes the following areas. Feature-list claims are not a substitute for device testing.

| Area | Cosmotune fit and suggested priority |
| --- | --- |
| Detailed quality label, source health, fallback transparency | Highest priority. Expose facts the decoder knows and diagnose why a preferred source failed. The 1.0.4 work already adds a compact format line. |
| Provider home shelves and local learning | High-value prototype after current release. Reuse our small model and existing catalogue rather than bundling a new global dataset. |
| ReplayGain/EBU R128 scanning, multiband EQ | Compare current normalization/DSP first; test clipping, bypass and channel balance. Do not silently alter the sound to make a quality badge appear better. |
| USB bit-perfect, exclusive DAP output, native DSD | Dedicated audio project. Android version/device/DAC support differs; desktop CoreAudio/WASAPI/ALSA paths need separate validation. Lossless decoding alone does not prove bit-perfect DAC output. |
| Local duplicate review, metadata editing, M3U8 export | Useful library improvements without a hosted backend. Preview writes and preserve original tags/files. |
| SMB, WebDAV, HTTP and Subsonic/OpenSubsonic libraries | Optional user-owned music sources. Share models and UI, isolate credentials and transport; these require a user's reachable server/share. |
| Word-timed lyrics, translation, romanization and sharing | Compare with our existing implementation and language/sync fixes. Additional providers need strict recording and language matching, plus editable offsets. |
| Rewind, listening statistics and backups | Extend existing replay/stats with portable versioned exports; avoid duplicate counting across pause, seek, cached playback and devices. |
| Concerts, music haptics, widgets, themes, large-screen layouts | Later enhancements, each requiring API/device/accessibility checks. Preserve Cosmotune's current design and reduced-motion choices. |
| Full/Lite distribution | Worth a package-size experiment only after measuring which optional native components dominate. A second flavour adds release/support cost. |

## Proposed sequence and boundaries

1. Finish and verify the pending 1.0.4 native feature/fix integration, including artist motion/signatures and format display.
2. Prototype the home-feed contract and tiny local preference learning outside the shipped feature path. Compare against our current ranking on speed, relevance, freshness and repeat rate.
3. Validate one authenticated or user-owned lossless source end to end on Android and each desktop target, including failures, expiry and seeking. Keep source and output quality distinct.
4. Add network-library and library-management features according to measured demand. Treat exclusive/DSD output and a general Mornye runtime as separately scoped work.

No arbitrary model/package-size estimates are established for importing the Mornye runtime. Its current licence is [LGPL-3.0-only with incorporated GPLv3 terms](https://github.com/mornyeapp/Mornye/blob/fb4945be910b0a7a8cce25eab3d1b3d80b3541e4/LICENSE), and the extension repository carries its own notices. Older indexed descriptions saying MIT do not describe the inspected revision. Preserve applicable licences, notices and corresponding source when adopting code.


## Implementation follow-up (2026-10-24)

The earlier review is a source audit. The current shared provider implementation, live public JioSaavn metadata/AAC decoding, Android emulator runtime proof and remaining compatibility gaps are recorded in [PROVIDER_PACKAGES.md](PROVIDER_PACKAGES.md). Music download recovery/pacing and local preference learning also now have implementations and separate evidence records. Editable HTTP add-on settings are implemented on Android and desktop; real HTTP tests verify saved values and preserve connection quality policy. This follow-up does not claim the full upstream application or every provider has been device-tested.
